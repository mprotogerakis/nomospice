LTspice is a trademark of Analog Devices, Inc.; Micro-Cap of Spectrum Software. nomospice is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by them, and ships none of their files: it reads and writes the LTspice file formats for compatibility. Origin of nomospice's own parts: its built-in symbols are drawn for nomospice; only what an .asc file depends on follows LTspice's symbols of the same name (pin positions, pin names and order, attribute names, where their labels sit). Its default device models (1N4148, 2N3904, 2N3906) are its own estimates from public datasheet values (forward voltage, current gain, transit frequency, junction capacitances, recovery time), not taken from a vendor's or LTspice's library; its op-amp, logic-gate, switch and line models are written for nomospice. Model libraries found on the computer (LTspice's, a Micro-Cap install) are listed switched off: they come with their own terms, and the user decides whether to use them. nomospice passes no model library's text to AI clients (MCP).
